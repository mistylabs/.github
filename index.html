Misty

A gentle guardian for your thoughts.

A manifesto

Misty is a secure gateway. It helps you sync, manage, share, and publish your personal notes, journals, and ideas. Without becoming the place where they live.

Your thoughts never become a product. They remain yours, always.

What Misty does

  • Sync your notes across all your devices, securely and privately
  • Keep your journals, diaries, and vaults organized in one place
  • Share thoughts with people you trust, but only when you want to
  • Publish selected notes as blogs, completely on your terms
  • Push your writing to dev.to or other platforms with one click

All of this, without ever claiming ownership of your words.

How it works

  • Everything stays on your device first. Your notes live with you, not on our servers.
  • We use zero-knowledge encryption. We literally can't read what you write, even if we wanted to.
  • Everything is private by default. You decide what to share, and when.
  • Publish only when you're ready. No pressure, no deadlines.
  • Works with the tools and workflows you already love.

Misty doesn't cage your thinking inside a platform. It follows your ideas wherever they already live.

This is not another editor. We love the tools you already use. Misty doesn't replace them. It walks beside them.
